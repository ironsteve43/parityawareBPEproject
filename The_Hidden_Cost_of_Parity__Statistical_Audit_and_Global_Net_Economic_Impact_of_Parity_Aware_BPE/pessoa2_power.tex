\subsection{Statistical Power \& Tokenization Failure Modes}
\label{sec:power_and_failures}

Failure to reject the null ($p > 0.05$) does not prove equivalence without adequate power. Following Miller (Section~5)~\cite{miller2024adding}, we compute the Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE) at $80\%$ power ($\alpha=0.05$): $\mathrm{MDE} = (z_{1-\alpha/2} + z_{1-\beta})\,\mathrm{SE}_{A-B,\mathrm{clustered}}$.

\textbf{Severe Benchmark Under-Powering:} \textbf{22 out of 22 languages ($100\%$)} are underpowered to detect a $1.0\%$ or $2.0\%$ accuracy degradation. Clustered MDE averages $\mathbf{\pm 9.88\%}$ across languages (Thai: $\pm 5.58\%$; Korean: $\pm 17.75\%$). Achieving $\mathrm{MDE} \le 2.0\%$ requires $N_{\mathrm{req}} = 6{,}723$ clustered questions per language---a $\mathbf{26.8\times}$ sample deficit over~\cite{foroutan2026parity}.

\textbf{Morphological Failures in Specialized Domains:} Executing the BPE algorithm with \texttt{Whitespace()} pre-tokenization reveals that Phase-2 \textit{fair-max} merge reallocation starves technical subwords (Table~\ref{tab:failure_modes}):
(1) Identifiers (\texttt{calculate} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{c|al|c|ul|at|e}) and JSON keys/enums (\texttt{transaction}, \texttt{PENDING}) shatter into character fragments ($+62.5\%$ to $+126.1\%$), inflating KV-cache footprint~\cite{petrov2023language,ahia2023all};
(2) Multi-digit constants fragment into individual digits ($+94.7\%$), degrading arithmetic alignment~\cite{singh2024tokenization};
(3) STEM terminology explodes by $+330\%$ (\texttt{neuro|degenerative} $\rightarrow$ 12 tokens), destroying morphological coherence.
